# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (user choice). One page, `index.html`, with styles in `assets/styles/` and scripts in `assets/scripts/`. Leaflet 1.9.4 from unpkg for the map.

## Users

Homeowners in South Dublin, Wicklow and North Wexford, in two equal situations:
- something has gone wrong (leak, no hot water, burst pipe, broken boiler or pump) and they need to reach a plumber quickly, often from a phone;
- they are planning work (cylinder replacement, heating, new taps or showers) and are checking whether this is a plumber they can trust.

## Product Purpose

A one-page website for Darling Plumbing, a local plumbing and heating business. Success is a phone call: the visitor finds the number, trusts the business and rings.

## Positioning

A father-and-son business (Andy and Brendan) with over 30 years in the trade, covering every kind of plumbing and heating job, available 24/7, with a perfect 5.0 rating from 54 Google reviews that repeatedly name them personally.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Contact is by phone only: 085 234 7259 (tap-to-call `tel:+353852347259`). No email, form or booking system.
- Open 24 hours, 7 days a week; emergency 24/7 support.
- Address: Wicklow Heights, Ballynerrin Lower, Wicklow, Ireland (52.9749543, -6.0472247). Google Maps: https://maps.app.goo.gl/LWpLhpi5jBR45AebA
- Services: general plumbing (electric showers, taps, sinks, toilets) and plumbing & heating (heating systems, cylinder replacement, pumps, radiators, emergencies).
- Page sections are fixed at five: hero, services, about, reviews, contact, plus header and footer. No extra content sections.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Darling Plumbing.
- Brand colours: #f9ff00 (bright yellow), #38494d (dark slate), #0174ca (blue).
- Voice: plain, friendly, first person plural, like the owners talking ("just give us a call", "no job too big or small").
- Layout: the user chose the conventional local-tradesperson page over concept directions (2026-11-22) and asked to "keep it simple but elegant" (2026-11-23). Identity comes from copy, type and the brand colours, not from a themed concept.

## Evidence on Hand

- Seven real Google reviews, quoted in `index.html`; overall 5.0 from 54 Google reviews.
- One photo: `assets/images/cylinder_orig.jpg` (600×800, a hot water cylinder install in a hot press).
- No logo, no team photos, no service photos, no accreditations. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. The phone number is never more than a glance away.
2. Real proof (named reviews, 30+ years, real photo) over marketing claims.
3. Sound like the owners, not an agency.
4. Keep it simple: one page, five sections, nothing added for decoration.
